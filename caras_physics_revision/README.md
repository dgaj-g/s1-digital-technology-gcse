# Cara's Physics Revision

A bespoke, auto-marking revision guide built around the topics in Cara's own
physics notes.

## Topics covered

1. Energy (types, conservation)
2. Renewable & non-renewable energy
3. Weight, work & energy (PE, KE, dropping objects)
4. Power & efficiency
5. Hooke's Law
6. Pressure
7. Moments & stability
8. Mass, density & states of matter
9. Motion (speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs)
10. Atoms & isotopes
11. Radioactivity (alpha/beta/gamma, ionisation)
12. Half-life & background radiation
13. Nuclear fission & fusion

## Question types

- Multiple choice
- Fill in the blank
- Short answer (keyword-marked with a model answer)
- Numerical calculation (with tolerance)
- True / false
- Sort & match (drag-and-drop with tap-to-place fallback)

## Tech

Pure vanilla HTML / CSS / JavaScript in a single self-contained file —
no frameworks, no images, no external dependencies. Best score per topic
is saved in `localStorage`. Mobile responsive.
