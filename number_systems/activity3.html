Conversion Arcade

One minute. As many conversions as you can. Build a combo by answering correctly in a row to multiply your points — but one wrong answer breaks the chain!

How to play:
• You have 60 seconds.
• Each correct answer scores points × your combo multiplier.
• Every 3 correct in a row raises the multiplier (up to ×5 🔥).
• A wrong answer resets your combo to ×1.
Quick reference
Binary place values: 128 64 32 16 8 4 2 1
Hex digits: A=10 B=11 C=12 D=13 E=14 F=15
Hex→denary: left×16 + right  •  Denary→hex: ÷16 (whole=left, remainder=right)
Binary↔hex: each group of 4 bits = one hex digit
